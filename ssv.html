About Us

Your Trusted Partner for Electrical & Home Solutions

Established in 2017 by Mr. Saridi Madhava Rao, SSV Agencies is a leading provider of premium electrical and home improvement products for homes and businesses.

Our wide range includes inverters, smart lights, energy-efficient bulbs, pumps, coolers, heaters, and durable appliances — all at competitive prices.

We also offer quality piping, secure windows and doors, and elegant conservatories, combining style and reliability under one roof.

At SSV Agencies, customer satisfaction, product quality, and trusted service are at the heart of everything we do.
